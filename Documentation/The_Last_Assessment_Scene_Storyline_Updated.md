## Scene 19 — The Continuity Grid

Q19 · The reveal

The protagonist connects the glitches, modified records, impossible timestamps, and the Assessor’s restrictions. By examining the evidence, they uncover the truth: the assessment chamber and memory sequences exist inside Eterna’s Continuity Grid.

The memories shown during the assessment have been reconstructed from stored data, partial recollections, and Eterna-controlled records. Some may be accurate; others may be incomplete or deliberately altered.

The simulation is used to evaluate a consciousness before memory restoration. Eterna has also turned it into a tool of control. The protagonist’s assessment was manipulated to prevent the recovery and release of evidence.

The Assessor was not necessarily lying. It had been instructed to treat an incomplete, altered record as authoritative.

Purpose: Deliver the central reveal through the protagonist’s own investigation. The story has not confirmed the simulation before this point.
