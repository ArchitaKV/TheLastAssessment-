# The Last Assessment — Initial Design Outline

This outline records only details established by the Story Bible. See `../The_Last_Assessment_Story_Bible.pdf` for the complete narrative source.

## Premise and assessment

Eterna preserves memories across lives through an experience-credit allocation system. The protagonist is a former Eterna memory auditor who investigated knowledge extracted from memories and sold to wealthy clients, including non-consensual cases. The Continuity Grid is a simulation used to evaluate minds before memory restoration. Its records may be altered or incomplete. The Assessor AI follows procedure and can compare evidence with records, but it is not omniscient; the assessment is monitored by Eterna.

The game is a first-person psychological science-fiction mystery. Its core structure is five acts and 20 main questions. Player history, evidence discovery, hidden traits, and decisions across the assessment affect outcomes; the last answer alone does not determine the ending. Do not expose numeric trait scores or eligibility hints.

## Act and question ranges

- Act I — Intake: Questions 1–4; values and memory allocation, memory-worth fragments, the 143-credit valuation, and a childhood memory voice.
- Act II — The Life You Lived: Questions 5–8; dismissal and access revocation, apartment promise, a locked personal record/regret, and changing a prior life.
- Act III — Missing Records: Questions 9–12; restricted-facility logs, engineer consent, missing-years contradiction, and a recording about access to the protagonist's records. Preserve the original file as evidence.
- Act IV — Forbidden Truth: Questions 13–16; the auditor role, memory-theft evidence, valuation manipulation, uncertainty around the final night, and the monitored Assessor. After Question 16, reveal that the Continuity Grid is a simulation and that records may be altered or incomplete.
- Act V — Final Judgment: Questions 17–20; fairer memory-system alternatives, evidence and infrastructure, discovery of a hidden preservation protocol, and Eterna's offer to suppress evidence in exchange for memory restoration. The offer may be accepted strategically. The final choice is judged in context of the full history.

## Ending names

The Bible establishes six endings: **Continuity Granted**, **The Quiet Erasure**, **The Escape Protocol**, **The New Assessor**, **The Archive Falls**, and **The Uncounted** (true ending). The Uncounted requires original evidence, conspiracy evidence, a manipulated assessment, discovery of the preservation protocol, and a challenge to Eterna's exclusive control while preserving the archive.
